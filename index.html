Origins

My curiosity began with video games—how they worked, broke, and came to life. Raised by immigrant parents with limited guidance, I found my footing through hands-on creation, hackathons, and internships at the Urban Assembly Gateway School of Technology

Foundation

I studied computer science, learning the fundamentals of systems, algorithms, and how computers truly operate beneath the surface. The rigor pushed me into increasingly challenging internships that tested my resilience and sharpened my technical confidence.

Momentum

Today, I drive impact within my organization while continuously expanding my skills in reliability, automation, and large-scale systems. Beyond work, I build personal projects and share my journey to inspire others navigating their own paths in tech.

Forward

Looking ahead, I aim to deepen my influence by designing data-driven, reliable solutions that scale and endure. My goal is to blend engineering excellence with thoughtful problem-solving to create meaningful, lasting impact.
